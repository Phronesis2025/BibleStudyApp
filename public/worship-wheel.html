Concept notes

The app now opens the way the finished product would. These are the notes that used to sit on its screens, plus what still needs a decision.

Open the app

How to try it

  • The first time you open the link, the intro plays (about a minute and a half). Then you rate your wheel, write your reflection, and land on Home.
  • What you enter is saved in this browser only, so opening the link again picks up where you left off. Nothing is sent to the church or anyone else.
  • To see the first launch again, use Settings › Your data › Erase everything, or “Start over as a new user” above. Settings › About › Replay the intro replays it without erasing anything.
  • The app follows your device’s light or dark setting. Settings › Appearance switches it by hand.
  • On a computer the app sits in a phone frame. On a phone it fills the screen. This page is reachable from the link under the phone, or from Settings › About.

Screen by screen

First-launch intro

  • Opens on the workbook cover. The cover’s red shrinks into the wheel and becomes Jesus’ slice, so His color carries from the cover to the slice to the center.
  • “Jesus is a helpful resource,” the workbook question about why Jesus stays a part instead of the center, then the reframe to “the all-sufficient source.”
  • Colossians 1:17–18 in one step, then two of the Session 1 questions. During each question, that area lights up with a bold red border so it stands out on the wheel. Then a demonstration of shading from the center out.
  • Each line stays up for a time based on its length. One faint bar along the bottom shows progress. Skip is in the top corner, and the round button at the bottom switches between light and dark.
  • It ends by gliding the wheel into its exact spot on the Rate screen.
  • Three captions were written for the intro and aren’t from the workbook: “Most of us give Jesus one part of our lives,” “Shade each area from the center out,” and “Let’s look at your wheel.”
  • Taken out to keep it short, and easy to put back: “Creator. Sustainer. Redeemer.”, Colossians 1:16, the workbook line about what the wheel helps us evaluate, the line “Next to family, work, media, finance and everything else,” and the question “Is this area fueling or distracting my joy in Jesus?”

Rate your wheel

  • Drag outward on a slice to shade it from the center, in 10% steps, or use the slider. A fuller slice means Jesus reaches further into that area.
  • Percentages keep it close to how most people shaded their wheels in class.
  • “Not sure?” opens the five questions printed under the wheel in Session 1.

Reflection

  • The Session 1 reflection questions and homework (“This Week’s Challenge”). The lowest area is picked for you.
  • The ruled lines copy the workbook’s write-in blanks.

Home

  • Your wheel stays in view. After your second check-in, a dashed outline shows the one before, so you can see change over time.
  • This week’s focus area and step come from your latest check-in.
  • The big red button opens the Delight Reset. Check in reopens the wheel with your last numbers filled in.
  • Lock screen makes a real wallpaper image of your wheel with Colossians 1:18. On iPhone, press and hold it, then Save to Photos.

Delight Reset

  • Tool 3 from Session 6, timed to 60 seconds: Breathe, Remember, Rejoice, Release. The steps move on by themselves, and Next skips ahead.
  • In Release, tap the area weighing on you and it fills from the center.
  • The closing line, “Joy grows where your attention goes,” comes from a note in the workbook margin and has no credit for now.

History

  • Each check-in saves a snapshot, like the workbook’s recap page, newest first.

Settings

  • Delight Reset nudges: 1–5 a day inside the hours you choose, at new random times each day, at least an hour apart. “Send a test nudge” shows what one looks like. In this concept it appears inside the app, while the finished app would send a real notification.
  • Sunday check-in: set for after church, with an evening reminder only if it hasn’t been done.
  • On iPhone, a web app can only send reminders after it’s added to the Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or later). “Show me how” walks through it.
  • Your data: everything stays on the phone, so Back up gives a code to copy and Restore takes it on a new phone. Erase everything starts over.

Design

  • Workbook details carried over: the “Session 0X” label beside a title and subtitle over a thin rule, the large mauve divider titles (“Delight 06”), verse references in the left margin, ruled write-in lines, underlined fill-in answers, and the black-line wheel with the red JESUS hub.
  • Fonts and colors are matched by eye from photos of the workbook. Nunito Sans and Merriweather Sans are the closest free matches. The real fonts and exact red should come from the church’s design files.
  • The CrossPoint logo is left out of this version until the church OKs its use.

Still to decide with the pastor

  • The workbook’s fonts and exact red, and the official logo files.
  • The app name (“Reframed Living”) and permission to use the class name, the Worship Wheel, the workbook content and the church logo.
  • Which Bible translation to use. The workbook’s Colossians text is ESV, and the Reading It Right card quotes the NIV.
  • Two or three verses per area, and the list of true things about Jesus for the Rejoice step.
  • The intro’s length and the three captions written for it.
  • Reminders: a web app added to the Home Screen, or an App Store app if reminders need to be more reliable.
